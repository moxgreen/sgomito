Goal. Get one of your pieces onto your opponent's home row: the colored edge at the top, with the dashed cells in your color.

Turn. Move two different pieces, each by one cell in one of the six directions. Orange opens the game with a single move.

Push. If the cell is occupied, by an opponent's piece or one of yours, you push that piece one cell further in the same direction and take its place. Only if the cell behind it is free: never two pieces at once, never against the wall, no captures.

No push-back. A piece that has just been pushed can't, in the very next turn, push back the piece that pushed it.

Ko. You can't recreate the position from the start of the previous turn: your opponent's turn can't be undone right away.

Draw. The same position three times, or 200 plies.

On the board

  • Click one of your pieces, then a highlighted cell. Or drag it.
  • Green: plain move. Red: push (the arrow shows where the pushed piece ends up).
  • Crossed out: move forbidden by the push-back rule or by the ko.
  • Gray piece: it has already moved this turn. You can undo the first move until you make the second.
  • Dashed arrows show your opponent's last turn.
